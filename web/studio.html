Studio Pro — User Manual & Guide

How to generate animated videos across Tech, Mind, Science & History

⚡ 1. Quick Start: 1-Click Auto Pipeline

  1. Type any topic in the Topic or Video Title box (e.g. Why You Overthink, How Elon Musk Got That Much Money, or What Did Ancient Humans Do).
  2. Select your preferred Neural TTS Voice (Christopher, Guy, Ryan, Jenny, Sonia).
  3. Click 🚀 Launch Studio Render. The engine researches the topic, records the voiceover, generates timed subtitles, maps characters, and outputs a finished MP4!

🎭 2. Character Action PNG Puppets

Drop your transparent character action PNGs into characters/ or click Upload PNG in the left sidebar. The AI reads each dialogue cue and matches the right character automatically!

🔊 3. Dialogue-Driven Sound Effects & Volume

Use the Emotion & Action SFX Volume slider in the left sidebar to fine-tune sound effect loudness (default: 15%). The AI synchronizes whooshes, cash registers, tension hits, dings, and clock ticks to spoken words!

📁 4. Video Files & Manual Mode

  • All finished videos are automatically saved in the output/ directory.
  • For manual videos with your own images, toggle 📁 Manual Assets Mode at the top, select your image folder, and upload your .srt subtitle file!
  • For complete detailed documentation, open USER_MANUAL.md in your project root.
